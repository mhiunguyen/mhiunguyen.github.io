CHAPTER 02 · NOV 2025 – APR 2026

The Turning Point: Building EcoBack

🏆 1ST PRIZE · NATIONAL SV_STARTUP VIII

EcoBack — Circular Packaging Return & Reuse

4-Member Team · Business Process Analysis & Web Prototype

🌐 Live Demo 📰 University News

E-commerce is booming in Vietnam, and with it comes a mountain of single-use packaging. Alongside three teammates, I asked: how can we connect businesses, consumers, and collection partners into a practical circular loop? That question took us from researching Vietnam's Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) policy all the way to the national stage.

01

Problem & EPR Research

Analyzed business requirements and Vietnam's EPR framework to pinpoint real operational bottlenecks in packaging recovery.

02

End-to-End Workflow Design

Translated complex requirements into operational workflows covering packaging tracking, collection, consumer incentives, and reuse.

03

Prototype & National Recognition

Contributed to documentation and built a web prototype with QR scanning & point accumulation using GitHub Copilot — winning First Prize at both university and national SV_STARTUP VIII.
